# VeloxTube

## Summary

Remove distractions from YouTube with simple on/off controls. VeloxTube hides Shorts, ads, sidebars, buttons and other parts of YouTube without changing the videos themselves.

## Description

VeloxTube gives you a cleaner YouTube, one toggle at a time.

Open the toolbar popup and hide only the parts you don't want. No setup wizard. No complicated profile. The controls are grouped for the home feed, search results, sidebar, video page and channels, with a few extra masthead options for good measure.

The home feed can lose Shorts, the topic bar, YouTube Playables, suggested subscriptions, featured content, sponsored cards and the full recommendations grid. Search results can be stripped of Shorts, topic filters, channel results and AI-generated video summaries. Handy if you want search to behave more like, well, search.

The sidebar doesn't have to stay crowded. Hide its Shorts link, subscriptions menu, footer links, Explore section or “More from YouTube” section. On the masthead, VeloxTube can remove the YouTube logo, notification bell and microphone button. It won't sign you out or replace YouTube's navigation with a custom app.

Watching a video? Turn off the Join and Thanks buttons, Share button, more-actions menu, live chat, comments, description or related videos. Channel pages can drop their wallpaper, Join button, featured content and Shorts section too. There are separate controls for those pieces, so you don't need to hide everything to hide one annoying element.

VeloxTube also has a toggle for sponsored cards inside feeds. That helps cut down the promoted stuff sitting between normal YouTube recommendations. It is not an ad blocker, though. It doesn't block or replace video ads, and it won't claim to do that.

Each setting works independently. You can hide Shorts on the home page but leave Shorts on channel pages. You can remove search channels without touching the sidebar. Changes save in the browser and apply whenever you open YouTube. Turn a toggle back off and VeloxTube puts the original page element back when possible.

By default, a setting change reloads the active YouTube tab. Don't want that interruption? Turn on “live changes” behavior by disabling “Reload tab on changes” in the popup. Your preference is saved locally. Theme changes don't reload the tab: choose System, Light or Dark from the popup's theme menu.

VeloxTube doesn't collect or sell personal data. It doesn't run analytics, tracking or remote logging. It makes no network requests of its own. Your toggles stay in local browser storage. The extension only reads YouTube pages and changes their interface; it does not read or store what you watch, your account details or anything else you enter on YouTube.

The only permissions are storage and access to YouTube pages. Storage keeps your switches and theme preference. YouTube access lets the content script hide and restore interface elements.

VeloxTube is an independent project. It isn't affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by YouTube or Google.
